PANOPTO LECTURE DOWNLOADER: HOW TO INSTALL (one time, about 1 minute)

Downloads HUJI Panopto lectures (camera + screen) so you can watch them offline,
with a player that swaps the two views and changes the speed.

1. Move this folder ("Panopto Lecture Downloader") somewhere permanent,
   for example your home folder or Documents.
   - NOT inside OneDrive, iCloud Drive or Dropbox: cloud folders can make the
     files "online-only" and then Chrome fails to load the extension.
   - Don't delete or move it later: Chrome runs the extension from this folder.

2. In Chrome, type   chrome://extensions   in the address bar and press Enter.

3. Turn on "Developer mode" (switch in the top-right corner).

4. Click "Load unpacked" (top left) and select this folder
   (the one that contains manifest.json). Click "Select".

5. Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin
   "Panopto Lecture Downloader".

Chrome may show a "developer mode extensions" notice now and then. That's normal
for extensions not installed from the Web Store; just dismiss it.


HOW TO USE

1. Log in to Panopto and open a lecture (the page where the video plays).
2. Click the extension icon, then "Download…".
3. Choose a folder and wait until both progress bars finish (keep the tab open).
4. Open the lecture folder and double-click player.html to watch.

Player keys: Space play/pause · S swap big/small · V change view ·
C camera only · X screen only · [ ] speed · ← → skip 10 s · F fullscreen.
You can drag the small video anywhere.

Saving lectures into OneDrive? Right-click the lectures folder in Finder and choose
"Always Keep on This Device", or the videos may not play offline.


UPDATING TO A NEWER VERSION

Replace the files in this folder with the new ones, then go to chrome://extensions
and click the reload arrow on the extension's card.

Use this only for lectures you have access to, for personal study, and in line
with university policy.
